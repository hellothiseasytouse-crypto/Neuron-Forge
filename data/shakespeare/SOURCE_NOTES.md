# Shakespeare text collection

The text files in this directory are stored locally and are used by the Neuron Forge corpus preparation and indexing scripts. The plays and poetry are organized as separate editable text files.

Source edition: The Tech's Complete Works of William Shakespeare archive, mirrored at https://github.com/TheMITTech/shakespeare and presented at https://shakespeare.mit.edu/.

Included in this selection:
- 37 plays listed in the source edition
- 154 sonnets
- A Lover's Complaint
- The Rape of Lucrece
- Venus and Adonis
- Funeral Elegy for Master William Peter (attribution has been debated)

This is a collected edition of surviving and attributed texts. No repository can contain lost works for which no complete text survives, and the authorship of some collaborative or disputed works is not settled.
