About Neuron Forge.

An independent experiment in building language technology from the ground up.

N

A from-scratch AI research project

Neuron Forge is an independent experiment in building AI technology from the ground up. The website currently uses a local conversation layer, browser-saved chat context, constrained arithmetic, and a searchable reference collection. It does not send conversations to a third-party model provider.

The main model target is Neuron Forge 250M: 248,144,832 parameters, 29 layers, 16 attention heads, and a 1,024-token context. Its architecture and GPU training workflow are in the repository, but no 250M weights have been trained yet. The previous 1M checkpoint is kept as a research experiment and is not used for chat. The 3B design is a separate future blueprint.
